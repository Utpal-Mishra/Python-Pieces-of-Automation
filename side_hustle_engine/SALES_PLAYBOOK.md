# Agency Sales Playbook

The engine automates research and production. Selling stays consultative and human-approved.

## Qualification before outreach

Contact a prospect only when the research pack shows a clear, explainable opportunity such as:

- no standalone website despite meaningful public demand/reviews;
- an existing site with obvious conversion or mobile/contact friction;
- strong social/local presence but no owned destination for enquiries, products or bookings;
- a niche where a clearer digital path can plausibly create measurable customer actions.

Do not contact a business merely because it appears in the database.

## Recommended outreach sequence

1. **Warm/contextual route first** — owner/manager introduction, in-person visit, local chamber/network, or social account where the business is active.
2. **Permission-based preview opener** — explain one observed opportunity and ask whether they want the private concept link.
3. **Send preview after interest** — keep the message short; the preview should do most of the explaining.
4. **15-minute discovery conversation** — confirm business goals, current customer acquisition, website/social pain points, desired actions and decision-maker.
5. **Offer one recommended package** — avoid presenting a confusing menu. Give a clear recommended option and one lighter alternative.
6. **Close around outcomes** — owned digital presence, easier customer action, measurable analytics and lower ongoing manual effort; never promise guaranteed revenue.

## Example opener

> Hi — I came across your business while reviewing independent local retailers. You have a strong local presence, but I could not find a standalone website that brings your collections, store information and customer actions together. I made a small private concept to show the opportunity. Would you be open to seeing the link?

## Discovery questions

- Where do most new customers currently find you?
- What do people most often message/call you to ask?
- Do you want the website primarily to drive store visits, enquiries/reservations, bookings, or online sales?
- Which products/services are most important commercially?
- Do you already use Instagram/Facebook/Google Business Profile/Shopify or another POS/e-commerce platform?
- What information is hardest to keep updated today?
- How would you know six months from now that the website was worthwhile?

## Commercial structure

Use pricing as a testable hypothesis and adjust from real close-rate/delivery data.

### Initial build

- **Presence site:** approximately €750–€1,200 for a focused local-business site.
- **Growth site:** approximately €1,200–€2,000 when catalogue, stronger integrations, analytics or richer content are required.
- **Commerce implementation:** quote separately when product catalogue, payments, shipping, inventory or Shopify work materially increases scope.

### Recurring plans

- **Website Care:** €59/month hypothesis.
- **Growth Intelligence:** €129/month hypothesis.
- **Commerce Intelligence:** €249/month hypothesis.

Do not discount recurring intelligence into meaningless 'maintenance'. Tie the subscription to measurable monitoring, reporting and action recommendations.

## Closing principle

The preview is not the product. It is evidence that we understand the business.

The product is:

**research + website + measurement + ongoing improvement**.
